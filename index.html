Course outline · 13 sessions

Financial Technologies
(FinTech)

Prof. Priyadarshi Dutta

Demonstrable skills

Upon completion of the course, students will be able to demonstrate:

  1. The ability to build prototypes and proofs of concept, applying FinTech concepts learned in class, that solve real-life business problems.
  2. A foundation for designing lending apps, including the type of information that should be collected from the borrower.
  3. Knowledge of the regulatory landscape governing FinTech in India.

Session map

01
Laying the foundation for the course 1 session

Why is this course being taught? What will be taught & how? How will assessments be made? How will this course help in professional life? Expectations from the students.

02
The fundamentals of FinTech 1 session

What is meant by FinTech? Origination and evolution across the globe. Why has it proliferated in recent times? A few global FinTech case studies.

03–04
Introduction to the concepts of Blockchain, Cryptocurrency, Encryption 2 sessions

Introduction to the concepts of Blockchain, Cryptocurrency, Encryption (Parts 1 & 2).

05–06
Introduction to the India Tech Stack 2 sessions

Introduction to the India Tech Stack (Aadhaar, UPI, DigiLocker) — Parts 1 & 2.

07
FinTech in India (Part 1) 1 session

Different types of FinTechs in India and overview of their business models. FinTech Lending: Difference between FinTechs, NBFCs and Banks. Concept of alternate data. Key trends in FinTech industry.

08
FinTech in India (Part 2) and commonly used Credit Risk metrics 1 session

Introduction to commonly used Credit Risk metrics and their applications — Delinquency rates, GNPA, NNPA, Provisions, Slippages. Concept of Cohort Analysis / Vintage Analysis.

09
How does a FinTech Lending Company operate? (Micro level view) 1 session

Functions of different departments, customer journey on the app, which department is responsible for what?

10
Recent innovations in the FinTech sector 1 session

Recent innovations in the FinTech sector (e.g. ULI, RBI Sandbox).

11
Group project
Blockchain use-case prototype · Group presentation 1 session

Each group builds a working web application that demonstrates an application / use case of the key concepts of Blockchain to solve a real problem. The problem could be anything a student identifies — even difficulties faced in hostel life — provided there is a reasonable use case for Blockchain in solving it.

12
Class activity
Regulatory environment and an RBI-styled supervisory exam simulation 1 session

Regulatory environment in the Indian FinTech space, followed by a simulation of an RBI-styled supervisory examination.

13
Wrap up 1 session

What were the key takeaways. Q & A session.
